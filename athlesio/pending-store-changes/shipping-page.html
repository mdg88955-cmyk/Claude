Where we ship

Austria and the EU · United Kingdom · United States

We do not currently ship to Canada. Creatine supplements sold in Canada require a Natural Product Number (NPN) from Health Canada, which this product does not yet hold.

Processing

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed after 14:00 CET on Friday are processed the following Monday.

Delivery times after dispatch

Austria 2–4 business days
EU 3–7 business days

We are still confirming realistic delivery windows for the United Kingdom and the United States with our carrier. We would rather leave those blank than publish a number we cannot stand behind. If you are ordering from either and want an estimate before you buy, email info@athlesio.at and we will tell you what we actually know.

Shipping costs

Shown at checkout before you pay. Free shipping thresholds apply per market.

Customs, duties and import VAT

Orders shipped outside the EU may be subject to import duties and taxes levied by the destination country. These are not included in our prices and are the recipient's responsibility. We cannot predict the amount — your local customs authority can.

If a parcel is refused at customs and returned to us, we refund the order value minus outbound and return shipping.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by email once your order ships.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your parcel hasn't arrived within a reasonable window after the estimated delivery date, email info@athlesio.at with your order number and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.
