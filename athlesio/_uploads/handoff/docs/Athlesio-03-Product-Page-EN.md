## Full description

**What it is**

A daily gummy built around 5,000 mg of creatine monohydrate, with BCAAs in a 2:1:1 ratio, L-carnitine, taurine, and vitamins B6 and B12. Sugar-free, vegan-friendly, berry flavoured.

Vitamins B6 and B12 contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism and to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.

**Why gummies**

Powder works. It also requires a shaker, water, and remembering. Most people who quit creatine quit because of the logistics, not the results — and creatine only does anything if you take it consistently for weeks. A gummy you can keep in your gym bag removes the excuse.

**How to take it**

Three gummies daily. Timing doesn't matter much — creatine works by saturating your muscles over time, not by giving you a pre-workout hit. Take them whenever you'll actually remember. Consistency over weeks is what counts.

Do not exceed the recommended daily dose.

**What's not in it**

No added sugar. No gelatin. No artificial colours. Non-GMO.

**Honest note on gummies**

Independent lab testing in 2025 found that a large share of creatine gummies on the market contained far less creatine than their labels claimed — creatine is chemically less stable in a gummy than in a powder. We think you should know that before you buy from anyone, including us. Ask any brand selling creatine gummies for a recent independent certificate of analysis. If they can't produce one, that tells you something.

## FAQ

**How long until I notice anything?**
Creatine works by saturating muscle stores, which takes roughly three to four weeks of daily use. There is no meaningful day-one effect. If a brand tells you otherwise, be suspicious.

**Do I need a loading phase?**
No. Loading protocols saturate stores faster; taking a standard daily amount gets you to the same place, just over a few more weeks.

**Will it make me gain weight?**
Creatine draws water into muscle cells, so some people see a small increase on the scale in the first weeks. It's intracellular water, not fat.

**Can I take it on rest days?**
Yes — daily consistency is the point. Rest days included.

**Is it vegan?**
Yes. No gelatin, and the creatine is synthesised, not animal-derived.

**Sugar-free — what's the sweetener?**
See the full ingredient list on the label. If you have a sensitivity to sugar alcohols, check it before ordering.

**How many jars for a month?**
One jar is 40 servings — about six weeks at three gummies daily.

**Do you ship to the UK?**
Yes. See Shipping for current times and any duties.

# Claims I deliberately did not write

Worth knowing what I left out and why, because these are what the Zendrop creatives lead with:

| Claim on supplier images | Why it's not here |
|---|---|
| "Boost energy and performance" (pre-workout) | Creatine does not work acutely. This is factually wrong, not just non-compliant. |
| "Replenish muscle creatine stores to promote recovery and growth" | Structure/function claim — US only, with disclaimer. Illegal in GB. |
| "STRENGTH / MUSCLES / ENDURANCE" | Non-authorised creatine claims in GB. |
| "8000 MG PER SERVING" as the headline | Implies 8,000 mg of creatine. It's 5,000 mg creatine in an 8,005 mg complex. Misleading by omission. |
| "Advanced Complex" | Meaningless. Kept only as part of the product name for supplier SKU matching. |
